We serve high-quality African & continental dishes in a stylish, inviting room — then turn the lights down and give Cape Town the nightlife experience it keeps coming back for.

306

Set in the vibrant heart of the CBD, 306 Lifestyle Lounge runs as two experiences under one roof: a kitchen built around bold, authentic African cooking alongside continental favourites, and a lounge rated among the city's best for atmosphere, sound and service.

Waterkant Street. Open every day, from lunch service through to last call.
